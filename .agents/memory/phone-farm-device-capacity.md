---
name: Phone Farm device capacity
description: The user's Phone Farm must not impose a fixed total device-slot limit.
---

Phone Farm device registration has no fixed total device-slot cap. The grid-size selection controls how many devices are visible on each page, not how many devices can be registered.

**Why:** The user explicitly said there should be no device-slot limit for them.

**How to apply:** Do not restore a fixed registration or deletion cap. Keep registration open-ended and provide paging when the selected per-page grid size is smaller than the registered device count.
