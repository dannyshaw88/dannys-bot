---
name: Follow tool detail overlays
description: Sources, Surplus, and Followed panels expand over Follow Users settings without covering neighboring tools
---

The Follow Users Sources, Surplus, and Followed panels use the same overlay interaction as Posted Media: their existing buttons stay in place, only one panel opens at a time, and the panel is positioned within the Follow Users settings container.

**Why:** These detail lists need more room without pushing or obscuring unrelated Human Session Tool settings.

**How to apply:** Keep the three controls on the Follow Users header, close the other two when one opens, and scope each absolute overlay to the Follow Users container rather than the whole Human Session Tool.
