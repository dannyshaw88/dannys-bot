---
name: Follow tool detail overlays
description: Sources, Surplus, and Followed panels expand over Follow Users settings without covering neighboring tools
---

The Follow Users Sources, Surplus, and Followed panels use the same overlay interaction as Posted Media: their existing buttons stay in place, only one panel opens at a time, and the panel is positioned within the full Follow Users tool container through Inject Browsing and Filters.

**Why:** These detail lists need more room without pushing or obscuring unrelated Human Session Tool settings, and empty states still need to occupy the entire Follow tool area.

**How to apply:** Keep the three controls on the Follow Users header, close the other two when one opens, and scope each absolute overlay to the full Follow Users container through the separator before Random Actions rather than the whole Human Session Tool.
